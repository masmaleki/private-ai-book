title: Quote policy (example)
acl: sales

Every quote line needs a margin of at least 12 percent unless a sales lead
approves an exception in writing. Quotes are valid for 14 days.

Offers from non-authorized brokers may be used only if the customer has
agreed to broker sourcing. Such lines must be marked in the quote.

An AI system may prepare a quote draft. Only a human with the sales lead
role may approve and send it. The person who created a draft cannot approve
the same draft.
